What we make

Three formats we are building first

We are starting with the formats we can take into a first pilot: cosmetic jars and lids, refill bottles, and stand-up pouches.

Cosmetic jars and lids

PHA or PLA with natural fibre. PHA is home compostable; PLA is industrially compostable. Certification of the finished pack is not done yet. Pilot fill sizes: 30 g, 50 g and 200–250 g. Screw lids are part of the pack.

Refill bottles

Sizes: 100 ml, 200 ml, 250 ml and 340 ml. The finished bottle material, cap and certification are still being confirmed.

Stand-up pouches

First size in development: 340 g. Other sizes on request. Intended with a resealable zip, and a degassing valve where the product needs one.

Coming later, or on request: tubes, droppers and tea-tin liners.

Why compostable

A better end of life, once it is certified

Plant-based materials

We use PHA or PLA with natural fibre. PHA is home compostable; PLA is industrially compostable. How a finished pack behaves depends on its exact makeup, thickness and the composting conditions that apply. Certification of the finished pack is not done yet.

Indian rules, followed carefully

India's Plastic Waste Management Rules require CPCB certification before a compostable-plastic claim can be made. We will help you with documentation once our finished packs are certified. We will not make claims about EPR treatment until then.

A story your customers can see

Packaging that can match your ingredients story, with natural colours, printing, foiling or labels — once the colours and finishes are confirmed for the certified pack.

Leaflane itself is not CPCB-certified or independently certified for compostability yet. We will not make compostability claims for our packs until the finished article is certified.

Questions

A short, honest FAQ

Can it hold water-based creams?

Not tested yet. Anhydrous balms and oils are the planned first fills. We will test water-based creams before we recommend the jars for them.

What is the shelf life?

Not tested yet. Shelf life depends on the product, the pack and how it is stored. We will run fill and storage tests with your product before any production run.

What is the heat limit?

To be confirmed. The finished packs have not been heat-tested yet, so we will not publish a temperature figure until we have one from those tests.

Is it food-safe?

The finished packs have not been food-contact tested yet. We will not claim food-safe use until those tests are done.

What is the material?

Jars are made with PHA or PLA with natural fibre. Both options are available. PHA is home compostable; PLA is industrially compostable. Certification of the finished pack is not done yet, and we will not make compostability claims until it is certified.

When will certification be done?

Certification is not done yet. Once the final material and design are frozen, testing and certification can take many months. We will not make compostability claims until the finished pack is certified, and we will share timelines with early-partner brands as that work is booked.
